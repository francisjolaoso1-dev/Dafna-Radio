WHO WE ARE

More Than Radio

Dafna Radio is a digital broadcasting and media platform created to inform, entertain, educate and connect people.

From community conversations and business opportunities to technology, entertainment and inspirational programming, Dafna Radio provides a platform where voices and ideas can be heard.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide credible information, meaningful entertainment and useful opportunities while giving individuals, businesses and communities a platform to be heard.

👁️

Our Vision

To become a trusted digital media and radio platform connecting communities in Abuja, Nigeria and beyond.

❤️

Our Values

Integrity, excellence, creativity, community, responsible journalism and respect for our audience.
